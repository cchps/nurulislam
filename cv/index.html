Professional Summary

Distinguished Professor with over 20 years of experience in geographic research, environmental science, and academic leadership. Expertise in Geographical Information Systems (GIS), climate change, and environmental management with a proven track record of cutting-edge research in floodplain and watershed management, climate change impacts on agriculture, and environmental components analysis. Published 52+ peer-reviewed articles in high-impact international journals. Successfully supervised 30+ graduate students (PhD, MPhil, MSc, and BSc levels) to completion. Served as Honorary Assistant Professor at the University of Nottingham, UK, and as reviewer for leading international journals.
